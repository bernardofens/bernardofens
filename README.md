### About Me

I'm a Computer Science student at PUCRS and a member of the Apple Developer Academy in Porto Alegre. I build iOS apps as part of a SCRUM team — start to finish, idea through App Store submission, not class projects.

Games are what pulled me into this field, and they're still what I care about most. That's why I spend as much time in **RealityKit**, **ARKit**, and **Blender** as I do in Xcode — I want the things I build to feel alive, not just functional.

### How I Work

1. **Design with a team** — At the Apple Developer Academy, I work in SCRUM sprints with designers and other developers, not alone.
2. **Build for real users** — Every project ends at submission, not a demo. That means handling edge cases, performance, and polish most student work skips.
3. **Add dimension** — When a feature calls for it, I bring in RealityKit or ARKit to make an interaction feel physical instead of flat.
